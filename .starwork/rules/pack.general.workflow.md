### General Workflow

1. Put source materials, reference links, and user-provided materials in `.superpowers/sdd/`; treat them as read-only by default.
2. Put AI-generated but unapproved work in `.superpowers/sdd/`.
3. Put user-approved outputs in `docs/`, or in another formal source declared by the project.
4. Do not silently treat drafts as final outputs.
